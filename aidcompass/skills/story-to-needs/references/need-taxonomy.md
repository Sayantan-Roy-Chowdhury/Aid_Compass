# Need taxonomy

- `food_support`: groceries, meals, pantry, community fridge, food delivery.
- `education_support`: tutoring, homework, study space, academic coaching.
- `youth_programs`: mentorship, recreation, after-school and peer programs.
- `employment_support`: resumes, interviews, job search, career navigation.
- `health_wellness`: non-emergency wellness navigation and general support.
- `housing_navigation`: housing information, referral navigation, shelter systems.
- `legal_information`: general legal information and qualified-professional referrals.
- `newcomer_support`: settlement orientation, language and local-service navigation.
- `transportation_support`: fare programs, accessible transit and trip barriers.
- `digital_access`: devices, internet, printing, computer access and digital skills.
- `family_support`: parent, caregiver, childcare and family-resource navigation.
- `disability_support`: accommodations, assistive technology and access navigation.
- `other`: use only when no listed category fits; include clear keywords.

Priority is based on time pressure and impact. `immediate` belongs to the safety gate,
not normal community-resource planning.
