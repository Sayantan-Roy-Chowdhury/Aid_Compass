# Data minimization guide

## Usually useful

- city or neighborhood
- broad age band
- support category
- urgency window
- preferred language
- online/in-person preference
- travel, schedule, document, and accessibility barriers

## Usually unnecessary

- exact home address
- full birth date
- full legal name
- school or workplace name
- income records
- immigration or medical records
- family conflict details
- passwords, government numbers, or banking details

When uncertain, leave the field blank and provide useful general matches first.
