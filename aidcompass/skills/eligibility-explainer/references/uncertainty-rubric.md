# Eligibility uncertainty rubric

- **likely**: all explicit age, location, and program conditions appear satisfied.
- **possible**: main conditions match; one minor fact or intake step needs confirmation.
- **uncertain**: an important condition is missing, ambiguous, or program-specific.
- **unlikely**: a supplied hard age, geography, or service condition appears not to match.

Forbidden wording: definitely eligible, guaranteed, approved, entitled, accepted.
Preferred wording: appears to match, likely, may qualify, confirm with the organization.
