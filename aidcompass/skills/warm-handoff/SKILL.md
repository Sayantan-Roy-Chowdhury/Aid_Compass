# Warm Handoff

1. Draft only for a selected resource.
2. State the service of interest and one or two practical questions.
3. Include only broad facts needed to ask the question.
4. Never claim eligibility or demand an outcome.
5. Never send the message automatically.
6. Prefer the user's requested channel; offer email or text when calls are a barrier.
7. Use `references/contact-tone.md` and the templates asset.

A good draft is short, respectful, and easy to edit.
