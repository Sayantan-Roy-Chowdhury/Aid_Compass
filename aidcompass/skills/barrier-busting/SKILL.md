# Barrier Busting

For each resource:

1. Compare the user's stated barriers with delivery mode, hours, documents, language,
   accessibility, cost, and contact fields.
2. Record barriers the resource clearly solves.
3. Record remaining barriers without blame.
4. Suggest only workarounds supported by the record or phrased as a confirmation question.
5. Score fit from 0 to 1.

Use `references/barrier-playbook.md` for patterns. Do not claim a required document can
be waived or a program can provide an unlisted accommodation.
