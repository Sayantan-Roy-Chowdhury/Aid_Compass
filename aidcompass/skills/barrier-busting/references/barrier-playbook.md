# Barrier playbook

- Transportation: prefer online, phone, delivery, closer sites, or one-trip plans.
- Documents: prioritize records with no listed documents; otherwise say “ask whether alternatives are accepted.”
- Schedule: prefer evening/weekend/online modes; confirm current hours.
- Language: match listed languages; otherwise ask about interpretation.
- Accessibility: match only listed features; never assume a building is accessible.
- Digital access: prefer phone or in-person; pair with a digital-access resource.
- Contact anxiety: prepare an email, short text, or phone script; do not auto-send.
- Cost: prefer free, then low cost; never infer hidden fees.
- Childcare: use only listed child-friendly features or ask the organization.
